# ADR-000: Decision Record Template

- **Status**: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-xxx
- **Date**: YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders**: names/roles

## Context

What forces are in play; what problem demands a boundary decision.

## Decision

The change we are making, stated in one sentence up front.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we commit to revisit and
when. Deviations from the engineering plan or the design doc must be
cross-registered in the deviation register (`docs/devlog.md`).

## Rejection notes

Alternatives considered and why they lost.
